How this trainer classifies flops

Dry vs Wet

How many strong draws and made hands the board allows right now. Points come from suits (two-tone +2, monotone +3) and connectivity (three cards within a five-rank straight window +3, two cards no more than two ranks apart +1). Paired boards take −1. 3+ points = Wet.

Static vs Dynamic

How much the best hand is likely to change on the turn. Points come from a flush draw that can still complete (two-tone +2), straight potential (+3 / +1 as above), and low boards where overcards change everything (high card 9 or lower +3, T–J +1). Paired −1, trips −2. 3+ points = Dynamic.
